## 8. Methodology References

- LST: Landsat 8/9 Collection 2 Level-2, ST_B10 band (scale factor 0.00341802)
- UHI: Pixel LST minus mean rural-reference LST (NDVI ≥ 0.35, NDBI ≤ 0)
- Spatial stats: Global Moran's I, LISA (Local Moran), Getis-Ord Gi*
- GWR: Geographically Weighted Regression via mgwr library
- HVI: AHP-weighted composite of exposure, sensitivity, adaptive capacity
- Priority zones: Weighted composite of LST, UHI, Gi*, population, NDVI, HVI
