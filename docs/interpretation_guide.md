# Interpretation Guide

High heat risk areas are locations where multiple signals overlap:

- High land surface temperature.
- Positive UHI intensity compared with rural reference pixels.
- Significant LISA High-High clusters or Gi* hot spots.
- High population or built-up density.
- Low vegetation, limited green space, or weak access to cooling features.

GWR coefficients should be interpreted locally. A strong positive NDBI coefficient in one area means built-up intensity is more strongly associated with heat there than elsewhere, not that the same relationship holds across the entire metropolis.
